Privacy policy — blizzlake-mail

Last updated: 2026-10-16

What the app does

blizzlake-mail is a private tool used only by its owner. It sends report emails from the owner's Gmail account to the owner.

Google user data

  • Permission requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send only — to send email on the owner's behalf.
  • Not accessed: the app does not read, search, store, modify, or delete any email, contacts, or other Google data.
  • Storage: the OAuth refresh token is kept only in the owner's local macOS Keychain. No data is stored on any server.
  • Sharing: no Google user data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
