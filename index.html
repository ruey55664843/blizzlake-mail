taroball-assistant

taroball-assistant is a private, single-user tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It sends automated report emails from the owner's bot account (blizzlake2025@gmail.com), and it keeps the owner's personal Gmail inbox tidy: it labels and archives advertising and scam email, files bills and bank notices under labels, and saves travel booking confirmations to the owner's Google Drive. It also helps the owner reorganize their own Drive files into a tidier folder structure, moving files only after the owner approves the plan.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
